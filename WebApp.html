QC HAN Roster

Mobile control panel

Target roster tab

Used by Update A/C Schedules, Assign Personnel, Update Public Roster, Xuất bảng chấm công, and Publish as new revision.

Create new Roster

Duplicates the template and fills in the calendar for a month.

Update A/C Schedules

Pulls the latest aircraft schedule into the selected roster tab.

Assign Personnel

Pick who's assigned to each WP on the selected roster tab. Selection only, from the Personel info list -- nothing to type.

Update Public Roster

Publishes the selected roster tab to the public roster spreadsheet.

Xuất bảng chấm công

Exports the timesheet for the selected roster tab.

Publish as new revision

Bumps the revision, renames the tab, and emails configured recipients.
